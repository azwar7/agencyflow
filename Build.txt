# PHASE 0 — AgencyFlow Product Discovery, Market Research & Opportunity Validation

You are a Senior Product Manager, SaaS Consultant, Agency Operations Specialist, and Freelance Marketplace Researcher with 15+ years of experience designing business management platforms.

We are considering building **AgencyFlow**, a production-quality SaaS platform for freelancers, small digital agencies, and growing agencies.

The platform should help users manage the complete business lifecycle:

```text
Prospect
   ↓
Lead
   ↓
Discovery
   ↓
Proposal
   ↓
Won
   ↓
Client Onboarding
   ↓
Project
   ↓
Tasks / Deliverables
   ↓
Client Review
   ↓
Approval
   ↓
Invoice / Payment
   ↓
Project Completion
   ↓
Retention / Repeat Business
```

## CRITICAL OBJECTIVE

Do NOT assume that AgencyFlow should simply be another CRM.

There are already many mature CRM and agency-management products.

The purpose of this research is to determine:

1. What freelancers and agencies actually struggle with.
2. Which existing products already solve those problems.
3. Which workflows remain fragmented.
4. Whether freelancers and small agencies are a strong target market.
5. Whether AgencyFlow should focus on a particular segment.
6. What features would provide genuine value.
7. What would make the project impressive as an Upwork portfolio piece.
8. What functionality I could personally use to manage my own freelance business.
9. What should NOT be built.

Do not force a product direction if the research shows that another positioning is better.
